About RPS

RPS Public School stands on the Dharuhera–Bhiwadi Expressway in Rewari district, Haryana, a CBSE-affiliated school founded in 2015.

This site pairs the school's published campus figures with an interactive 3D visualization, so families can get a feel for the spaces where learning, sport and play happen.
